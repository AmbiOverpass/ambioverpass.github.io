01Abstract

  • Problem. A vague map request yields many executable OverpassQL queries. Most of them, however, return empty or identical results, so clarification should follow what the queries return.
  • Benchmark. As no existing dataset supports this task, we build AmbiOverpass-Bench with 1,754 planning instances and 254 rewriting cases.
  • Method. Superset execution evaluates the candidates together and groups readings with identical results into result classes. On these classes, exact Shapley values credit each slot with its share of the joint gain, while a look-ahead planner chooses the next question and when to stop.
  • Result. The gains are largest on two-landmark requests. Without a question budget, AmbiOverpass improves utility by 12.3 points over information gain, and with three questions it further exceeds consistency- and LLM-based clarification by at least 21.2 points in accuracy.
Full abstract

Natural-language requests to OpenStreetMap often leave geographic areas, object tags, element types, landmarks, or radii unspecified, yielding multiple executable OverpassQL queries. Since many queries return empty or identical results, effective clarification depends on execution outcomes rather than textual ambiguity alone. We study execution-aware interactive clarification for Text-to-OverpassQL and construct AmbiOverpass-Bench, the first benchmark for this problem, comprising 1,754 planning instances and 254 rewriting cases. We find that existing clarification methods often select questions based on interpretation uncertainty or immediate gains, overlooking questions whose benefits emerge jointly. To address this limitation, we propose AmbiOverpass, an execution-aware framework that uses superset execution to efficiently evaluate candidate queries and groups execution-equivalent readings into result classes of an ambiguity topology. On this topology, exact Shapley values attribute joint clarification benefits to individual slots, while a look-ahead planner selects questions and determines when to stop, with a policy-improvement guarantee. Experiments show that superset execution reduces database requests from one per candidate query to one per candidate area and landmark kind. On two-landmark requests without a question budget, AmbiOverpass improves expected utility by 6.5 points over one-step planning and 12.3 points over information gain over readings. Under a three-question budget, it improves execution accuracy by at least 21.2 percentage points over consistency-based and LLM-based clarification.

02Many Queries, Few Different Answers

Although a vague request maps to many executable queries, most of them return empty or identical results. A useful question therefore separates different results, whereas a question that only separates query texts can leave the answer unchanged.

  1. Execute all candidatesOne superset query per area and landmark kind replaces one query per candidate.
  2. Merge identical resultsReadings with the same result fall into one class, so the planner only needs to separate classes.
  3. Ask by joint valueSince a question may pay off only together with others, Shapley values share out the joint gain, and a look-ahead planner then decides what to ask and when to stop.

Figure 2. Given the request “Places to power up my car in Bolivia?”, AmbiOverpass builds 32 candidates and executes them with one superset query per area. The 13 non-empty readings then merge into 9 result classes, on which Shapley values and a one-step look-ahead plan the questions.

Policy-improvement guarantee. Since the look-ahead planner can always fall back on the action of Shapley greedy, its expected utility is never lower than that of Shapley greedy under the same prior, answer model and question budget.

05Results

Where the gains come from

  • The advantage grows with ambiguity. On single-object requests at K = 2, AmbiOverpass and EC²-Greedy are nearly tied (0.881 vs. 0.880). On two-landmark requests at K = 3, by contrast, the gain rises from 3.2 points for requests with at most five slots to 7.0 points for those with six or seven slots.
  • Greedy planning mostly fails by stopping too early. Under a uniform prior, 95% of its utility loss on two-landmark requests occurs in states where it stops prematurely.
  • As a result, the gain over EC²-Greedy increases from 2.3 points at K = 1 to 6.5 points without a budget, which also exceeds information gain by 5.5 points.

09Efficiency

  • Superset execution cuts Overpass calls from 222.0 to 2.9 per single-landmark request, and the estimated time accordingly drops from 6,787 s to 96 s.
  • These savings arise because shared supersets are retrieved once and candidate results are rebuilt locally.
  • Planning stays light as well. Including exact Shapley computation, it takes 0.8 ms per single-object request and 37–60 ms per two-landmark request, since memoization compresses 642,607 question-history nodes into 1,962 distinct states.
